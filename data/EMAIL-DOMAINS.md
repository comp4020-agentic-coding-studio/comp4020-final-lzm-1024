# University email routing

Checked 1 October 2026. `campus.mjs` contains explicit accepted university domains and student aliases. University root domains also route to their university, including staff addresses; this does not distinguish students from staff or verify ownership of a mailbox. Unknown domains retain the existing manual school choice. Substrings and unlisted subdomains do not automatically match.

- ANU: `anu.edu.au`, documented by [ANU ID and password](https://services.anu.edu.au/information-technology/id-and-password).
- USYD: `sydney.edu.au`, `uni.sydney.edu.au`; the student format is documented by [Sydney Student IT](https://www.sydney.edu.au/students/student-it/email-wireless.html).
- UNSW: `unsw.edu.au`, `ad.unsw.edu.au`, `zmail.unsw.edu.au`; current staff/student login and alumni zMail are documented by [UNSW MyIT](https://www.unsw.edu.au/myit/services/account-access-email/email).
- UniMelb: `unimelb.edu.au`, `student.unimelb.edu.au`, documented by [Accessing student email](https://ask.unimelb.edu.au/faq/5635/accessing-student-email/).
- Monash: `monash.edu`, `monash.edu.au`, `student.monash.edu`; student format is documented by [Monash eduroam](https://www.monash.edu/esolutions/network/eduroam-wi-fi), alongside [Monash email](https://www.monash.edu/esolutions/email-collaboration/email). The university's legacy `monash.edu.au` web domain is also routed to Monash.
- UQ: `uq.edu.au`, `student.uq.edu.au`, `uq.net.au`, `uqconnect.edu.au`; current and earlier aliases are documented by [UQ student email](https://my.uq.edu.au/node/1926) and [Earlier UQ email formats](https://support.my.uq.edu.au/app/answers/detail/a_id/3298/related/1).
- UWA: `uwa.edu.au`, `student.uwa.edu.au`, documented by [Student email explained](https://ipoint.uwa.edu.au/app/answers/detail/a_id/1579/~/student-email-explained).
- Adelaide: `adelaide.edu.au`, `student.adelaide.edu.au`, `adelaideuni.edu.au`, `study.adelaideuni.edu.au`, plus the legacy UniSA domains `unisa.edu.au` and `mymail.unisa.edu.au`. [Adelaide transitioning students](https://adelaide.edu.au/students/transitioning-students/) describes the 2026 email transition and university addresses; [student email format](https://student.ask.adelaide.edu.au/app/answers/detail/a_id/1731/~/whats-my-student-email-address%3F) records `student.adelaide.edu.au`; [UniSA course communication](https://my.unisa.edu.au/public/CourseOutline/ViewCIB.aspx?id=39586&preview=n) documents `mymail.unisa.edu.au`. Legacy addresses route to the current Adelaide campus without changing existing fixed accounts.
