# Photographic test poster collection

Collected on 2 October 2026. The machine-readable [manifest](photo-gallery.json) contains 24 original CampusWall compositions, the exact photographer credit and source page for every image, and the official source for each campus venue.

## Photos and licence

All 24 images are Pexels photographs downloaded from their image CDN and locally served as WebP files. The [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) permits website use and modification, with attribution optional. CampusWall nevertheless provides source-page, photographer and licence links on every detail page. The download script resizes and compresses images; it does not depend on a third-party image host at runtime.

The photographs are illustrative, **not photographs of the listed campus venue**. Neither the photographers nor the universities endorse these fictional activities. No real organisation, ticket sales or university programme is attributed to a test event.

Map buttons use a Google Maps named-venue search with the university and campus for disambiguation.

## Publication and persistence

Each campus has 24 test posters (192 campus records, sharing 24 photo assets). The six exact original built-in samples are upgraded in place so saved references, comments and old URLs survive; other legacy or user-created notices are untouched. Missing samples are inserted with deterministic IDs. The seed is transactional and idempotent. Dates start the day after first installation in each campus timezone and are never silently refreshed at restart.

Every poster graphic and card says **TEST EVENT**. Details explain that the activity, date and programme are fictional and no venue is booked. Calendar exports prefix the title with **[TEST]**, retain the test description, and are tentative, transparent entries. Test notices have a disabled system author and cannot impersonate a real organiser in private messages.
