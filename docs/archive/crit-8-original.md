# Crit 8 reflection

I chose Campus Poster Studio because it makes the final project's shared state visible: a campus event poster begins as a group artefact and becomes a public one. I first treated poster creation as the site entry point, then corrected that after noticing it excluded the larger audience. Guests now enter through a university poster wall, while creation remains optional.

For this crit I built the smallest complete path: browse ANU posters as a guest, sign in with a pilot display name, make a draft with text and shapes, publish it, and revisit it. The deployment stores data on the supplied persistent volume. The next iteration will test two browsers editing the same draft and improve the rules for invitations and simultaneous edits.
