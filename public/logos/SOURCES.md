# University logo sources

Retrieved 1 October 2026, copied without redrawing or recolouring. These identify the selected campus; CampusWall remains a student project and does not verify university affiliation.

- ANU: `anu.svg` is the white/gold logo used by the [ANU website](https://www.anu.edu.au/) footer. Asset: https://d1blp3aor2iuhp.cloudfront.net/images/logos/logo-ANU.svg
- USYD: `usyd.svg` is the white logo published by the [University of Sydney website](https://www.sydney.edu.au/). Asset: https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/icons/logos/logo-usyd-white.svg
- UNSW: `unsw.png` is the white logo used by the [UNSW website](https://www.unsw.edu.au/) footer. Asset: https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/graphics/logos/unsw/UNSW_logo_white.png
- UniMelb: `unimelb.svg` is the default housed logo published by the [University of Melbourne design system](https://designsystem.web.unimelb.edu.au/style-guide/logo/), referenced in version 15.14.0's stylesheet. Asset: https://d2h9b02ioca40d.cloudfront.net/v15.14.0/assets/logo-9f61db642f53f8d36d9c.svg

University marks belong to their respective owners. Assets are served locally to avoid third-party image requests on campus pages.
